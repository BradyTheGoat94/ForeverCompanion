## Editorial decisions

- Most Wowhead pages explicitly say their level-60 priorities are speculative because beta was capped at level 30. All profiles preserve beta/not-live-tested labels.
- Fury: the Wowhead ordered list says 9% Hit while the explanation says 8%. The addon flags the discrepancy and does not assign a universal numeric Hit cap. Hunter and Retribution numeric Hit targets are also left to client/talent verification rather than copying one cap to every build.
- Frost Mage: the source assumes 5-6% talent Hit when listing 11-12% additional Hit. This assumption is stated. Spell Hit targets for other casters must also account for talents and enemy level.
- Rogue: ability Hit, poison Hit and dual-wield auto-attack Hit have different targets. The source calls Crit and Haste close in its prose, so they share a comparison tier. The source-specific 308 Weapon Skill target remains a reported beta reference.
- Feral damage: the source starts with percentage increments, not per-point weights. This is stated; its percentage order is not a score for whole items. Bear tank survival and threat are separate references. Guardian remains only an unconfirmed planner label.
- Protection Paladin: only the leading portion of the balanced list is summarized, explicitly labeled partial. Protection Warrior uses the original Icy Veins author's separate survival/threat advice. No universal defense requirement is imposed on every tank or encounter.
- Elemental and Warlock: other guides order stats differently. The addon identifies its chosen source and does not claim a consensus ranking. No simulation accuracy claim is inherited from the source.
- Spell Power, school-specific damage, talent conversions, enemy level, encounter length and actual item budgets make a simple ranking conditional. No live stat scan or automatic item scoring is added.
- No full verified BiS list is inferred from the existing eight loot records. Wishlist targets and obtained marks are user-entered notes, not upgrade or ownership detection.
